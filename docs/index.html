A thousand years of breakthroughs, and how fast they spread

Big inventions used to take generations to reach most people. Each wave has moved faster than the last. This page puts generative AI on that curve.

Not a like-for-like comparison: the older figures count households that own a technology, and this one counts adults who use AI at all. See method notes below.

      Method notes

      "Launch" uses the breakthrough dates listed above: telephone 1876, central power 1882, commercial radio 1920, public Web 1991, iPhone mid-2007, ChatGPT late 2022. Pick different launch dates and the curves shift.

      The generative AI survey counts anyone who uses it at all. That is a lower bar than having a phone line or electricity wired into the house. Read the gap between the curves as a sense of the trend, not a precise measurement.

      Data: Our World in Data, Technology Adoption · Bick, Blandin & Deming, Real-Time Population Survey, via FRED · each breakthrough links to its own source.
