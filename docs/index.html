A thousand years of breakthroughs, and how fast they spread

Big inventions used to take generations to reach most people, and each wave moved faster than the last. Generative AI started from a high base, but year by year it is spreading at about the same pace as smartphones and radio did.

      Method notes

      "Launch" uses the breakthrough dates listed above: telephone 1876, central power 1882, commercial radio 1920, public Web 1991, iPhone mid-2007, ChatGPT late 2022. Pick different launch dates and the curves shift.

      "Pace" is the steepest straight-line climb over any 18-month window, reading between the published data points. The older series are sampled every few years, so their short bursts are smoothed out. The AI survey only starts in mid-2024, so it can't show the first 18 months after ChatGPT launched.

      The generative AI survey counts anyone who uses it at all. That is a lower bar than having a phone line or electricity wired into the house. Read the gap between the curves as a sense of the trend, not a precise measurement.
